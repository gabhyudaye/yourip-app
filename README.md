# YouRip

A small Mac app that saves videos from sites like YouTube, Vimeo and Dailymotion. Three modes: **Best** (highest quality, converted to MP4), **Friendly** (MP4 that plays everywhere) and **Audio**. Finished downloads are listed under **Completed tasks**, where you can convert them to other formats.

## Download
Get **YouRip-Mac.zip** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).

Needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer).

1. Unzip it.
2. Double-click **RUNME.command** (if macOS blocks it, right-click it and choose Open). It installs what the app needs (yt-dlp, ffmpeg, deno) and opens YouRip.
3. Next time, just open **YouRip.app**.

## Please note
- Only download videos you have the right to save. Many sites forbid downloading in their terms.
- The app is not signed by Apple, which is why RUNME.command clears the "unidentified developer" warning for you.
- Windows is not supported.
